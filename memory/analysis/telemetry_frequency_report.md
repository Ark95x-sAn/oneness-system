## How the Math Works

```
leverage_score = value_potential / max(1, compute_cost)
```

- **value_potential** = how much annual cash, capability, or optionality the band protects/creates
- **compute_cost** = how much CPU/attention/effort it takes to act on that band right now
- Low cost + high protection = highest leverage
